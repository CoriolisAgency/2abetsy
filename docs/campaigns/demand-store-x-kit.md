## Engagement rules

1. Answer “what’s a good ___?” with **data + store link**, not spam.
2. Quote industry news only when you attach **your** chart or shelf.
3. Tag a manufacturer only when they organically top a section that day — factual, not cosplay partnership.
4. Never claim “official partner” until written.
5. Metric that matters week 1: **link clicks**, not follower vanity.
