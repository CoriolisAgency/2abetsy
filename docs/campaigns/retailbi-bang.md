## 5 Shorts (~30–45s)

### S1 — Weather is not the battle
- **Hook (0–3s):** “NICS is not how many guns sold.”
- **Columns:** light NICS; dim Index + Ask.
- **Beat:** Public FBI checks = weather. Useful. Not the register. Not the hunt.
- **Close:** “Three numbers. Stop making them one.”
- **Land:** FFLIntel issue 01
- **On-screen:** NICS / Index / Ask lockup. No product UI.

### S2 — Last season’s map
- **Hook:** “Last month’s sell-through is last season’s map.”
- **Columns:** light Index.
- **Beat:** A monthly same-store print is an after-action report. True. Late. The river moved.
- **Close:** “Read it. Don’t march like the ford is still there.”
- **Land:** fortress
- **B-roll:** Tzu still (`betsy-tzu-art-of-war.jpg`). No unboxing.

### S3 — The ask that never rang
- **Hook:** “The search that hit zero never becomes a unit.”
- **Columns:** light Ask.
- **Beat:** Someone asked. Stock said no. The register stayed quiet. That still happened.
- **Close:** “Unmet demand is terrain. Not a rounding error.”
- **Land:** fortress

### S4 — Folded into one headline
- **Hook:** “The industry keeps adding three numbers and calling it demand.”
- **Columns:** all three pulse, then smash into one smeared bar labeled “demand.”
- **Beat:** Checks. Units. Asks. Different raw material. Collapse them and you plan from a myth.
- **Close:** “Keep the columns.”
- **Land:** FFLIntel issue 01

### S5 — Scout vs after-action
- **Hook:** “Know the terrain before the register rings.”
- **Columns:** Index = after-action; Ask = scout.
- **Beat:** Closed units tell you where the army camped. The ask is the column still walking.
- **Close:** “Art of Demand. Fog of sell-through.”
- **Land:** fortress (essay mention in pinned comment only: 2abetsy.com/art-of-demand-retailbi)

**Do not film:** box-opening, AXIS/RetailBI UI tours, “we replaced their dashboard,” fake Index prints, invented percents.
